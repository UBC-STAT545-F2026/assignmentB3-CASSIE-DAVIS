## Fetch my Match :)

Fetch my Match :) is an R Shiny application made to match users with dog breeds by answering 10 questions about their lifestyle.

#### Feature 1: Reactive top 10 plot

A horizontal bar chart shows your 10 highest matches, updating automatically when answers are changed

#### Feature 2: Reactive top 50 table

A table showing your 50 highest matching breeds to allow you to explore traits more individually.

#### Feature 3: Personalized top match

Text identifying your highest ranking dog breed displays the compatibility score out of 50, updating automatically.

#### How to use the app:

1. Answer lifestyle questions in the sidebar
2. Review your top match breed, explore your top 10 plot, and check the top 50 to see your compatibility on individual traits.
3. Change your answers to see how breed recommendations change

Test the app out here! [Try_Fetch_my_Match_:)](https://cassied-cloud.shinyapps.io/fetch_my_match/)

#### Data and limitations

This app uses the dog_ratings.csv dataset, which has breed-level characteristics and ratings, and compatibility scores were calculated using a custom points system.

The recommendations are for general exploration, and definitely aren't guarantees of an individual dog's temperament or health. Users should also research individual dogs and use responsible adoption practices before making a decision.
